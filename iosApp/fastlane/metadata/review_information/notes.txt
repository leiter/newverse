No account is required to review this app. On the login screen, tap
"Als Gast fortfahren" (Continue as guest) to access the full product
catalogue, product details and the basket.

The app is a pre-order tool for a weekly organic farmers market in
Fürstenwalde/Spree, Germany. Customers browse products and pre-order
them; pickup is always on Thursday at the market stall. Orders can be
edited until Tuesday 23:59 before the pickup day. No payment is taken
in the app -- customers pay at the stall on collection.

The user interface is German only, as the app serves a single local
market.
