No account is required to review this app. On the login screen, tap
"Continue as Guest" ("Als Gast fortfahren" in German) to access the full
product catalogue, product details and the basket.

The app is a pre-order tool for a weekly organic farmers market stall in
Fürstenwalde/Spree, Germany. Customers browse products, choose the market
they collect from and pre-order for its next market day (currently
Thursday). Where the market offers it, customers can choose delivery
instead and enter a delivery address and preferred time. Orders can be
edited until Tuesday 23:59 before the market day. No payment is taken
in the app -- customers pay on collection or delivery.

The app is available in German and English and follows the device
language.

New in 1.0.3: when the search field on the home screen is tapped, the
large product image at the top folds away so more results are visible.
